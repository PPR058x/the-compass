# assets/images/press/

Home for EPK-facing media referenced by `epk.html`. Nothing here is auto-generated — drop finished files in and update the corresponding markup in `epk.html`.

## `photos/`

High-resolution press photography.

- Format: `.jpg` (web/print use) at a minimum 300ppi-equivalent resolution for print use by media.
- Naming: `ppr-press-<short-description>-<number>.jpg` (e.g. `ppr-press-portrait-01.jpg`).
- Once a file is added, replace the corresponding placeholder tile in `epk.html`'s "High-Resolution Press Photos" section (`#photos`) with an `<img>` and enable its "Download Photo" button (remove the `disabled` attribute).

## `logos/`

Brand/logo files for press and partner use.

- Format: transparent `.png` (screen) and `.svg` (scalable) where available.
- Once finalized, link them from `epk.html`'s "Brand Assets / Logos" section (`#brand-assets`) and remove the pending notice there.

## Notes

- Do not add files here that haven't been approved for public/press distribution.
- Keep filenames stable once published — outlets may link directly to these paths.
