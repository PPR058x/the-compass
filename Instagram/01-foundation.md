## 3. Reel — "Emergence" Atmosphere Loop

**Type:** Reel
**Brand pillar:** Light / Frequency
**Concept:** A slow, meditative loop establishing the visual world — no narrative, no product, no CTA beyond presence.

- **Pacing:** Slow and deliberate throughout, 10–15 seconds, designed to loop seamlessly. No cuts faster than ~2–3 seconds; prefer continuous slow push/pull camera moves over hard cuts.
- **Key visual beats:**
  1. Total black frame, near-silent.
  2. A single point of gold light appears center-frame and slowly widens into a rim-lit silhouette or geometric form.
  3. Fine smoke or particle drift crosses the frame, catching the gold light.
  4. Slow pull-back to reveal thin gold geometry (a single motif only, per §6 "one geometry motif per composition") framing the subject.
  5. Fade to black, loop point.
- **On-screen text:** None, or a single restrained line appearing in the final second: "The World Is Changing…" (used exactly as written, no addition).
- **Sound direction:** Ambient/atmospheric original score — low drone, soft resonant tone, subtle frequency-adjacent sound design. **No album audio** (none exists publicly yet; this phase is 12+ weeks pre-release).

**Visual asset requirement:** Motion loop per `BRAND_BIBLE.md` §7 motion rules (smoke, light rays, particles, slow/deliberate). *(Asset not yet produced — placeholder.)*
