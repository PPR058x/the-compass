## 3. Reel — Slogan Assembling From Particles

**Type:** Reel
**Brand pillar:** Frequency / Light
**Concept:** A slow particle-assembly sequence that gradually forms the second primary slogan — the "partial reveal" happens in the visual build, not in the wording itself (the slogan text, once it appears, is exact and complete).

- **Pacing:** Slow and continuous, 12–18 seconds. No hard cuts — one unbroken shot or a single slow cross-dissolve at most.
- **Key visual beats:**
  1. Black frame; faint gold particles drift in from the edges.
  2. Particles begin gathering loosely near center, echoing a golden-ratio spiral path per `BRAND_BIBLE.md` §6.
  3. Particles slowly resolve into legible type: "Can You Feel The Frequency?" — assembly should feel organic and unhurried, not a fast typewriter effect.
  4. Hold on the fully formed line for 2–3 seconds before a slow fade.
- **On-screen text:** The slogan itself, formed by the particle animation described above. No additional captions or lower-thirds.
- **Sound direction:** A single sustained tone or soft resonant pad that swells as the particles converge, resolving to near-silence at the hold. Original sound design only — **no album audio**.

**Visual asset requirement:** Particle-assembly motion graphic, gold particles on black, per §6 golden-ratio spiral guidance and §7 motion rules. *(Asset not yet produced — placeholder.)*
