## 3. Reel — "The Pattern Completing"

**Type:** Reel
**Brand pillar:** Frequency / Awakening
**Concept:** The symbolic countdown motif — geometry completing itself — used across the final stretch instead of numeric day-countdowns framed as urgency.

- **Pacing:** Slow and continuous, 10–15 seconds, loopable. One unbroken build, no fast cuts.
- **Key visual beats:**
  1. An incomplete gold-line geometric form (e.g., Metatron's Cube missing several lines) sits on black.
  2. Over the duration, remaining lines slowly draw themselves in, one at a time, unhurried — each line arriving like a soft pulse of light rather than a snap.
  3. On the final line completing, a brief warm bloom of light across the full form.
  4. Hold on the completed geometry.
- **On-screen text:** None during the build. On the final hold: "The pattern is almost complete." (Symbolic framing, not a numeric countdown — consistent with `CLAUDE.md` §6 "no countdown/urgency language framed as pressure.")
- **Sound direction:** A single sustained tone that resolves/consonant-resolves exactly as the geometry completes — original sound design. **No album audio** (still pre-release).
