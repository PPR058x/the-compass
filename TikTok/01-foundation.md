# Phase 1 — Foundation (4 May – 23 May 2026)

**Cadence:** 2x/week on TikTok. **Album audio:** not permitted — original sound design/frequency tones only. **Album mention:** none — zero direct promotion, no "album coming" framing. This phase exists purely to seed the black-and-gold, sacred-geometry visual world and let a consistent aesthetic register before anyone knows what it's for.

Below are 3 representative video concepts illustrating the phase's cadence and goals (aesthetic consistency, early saves/shares, follower quality over quantity).

---

## Video 1 — "The Line Appears"

**Hook (0–3s):** A single thin gold line traces itself across a black screen in silence, then a low, deep hum begins.

**Beat-by-beat script:**
- **0:00–0:03** — Full black frame. A single gold line (1–2px equivalent) begins drawing itself from the center outward, tracing the first arc of a Flower of Life motif. No music yet, just room tone fading into a low sub-bass hum. On-screen text: none yet — let the visual breathe.
- **0:03–0:08** — The line continues, slow and deliberate, completing one full circle. A second circle begins overlapping it. Hum swells almost imperceptibly.
- **0:08–0:14** — Full Flower of Life pattern completes in gold light, glowing faintly against the black. On-screen text fades in, lower third, ivory serif: *"Some patterns take time to see."*
- **0:14–0:18** — Slow pull-back reveal — the geometry is glowing on what appears to be skin/a surface in shadow (implies a person, unrevealed). Sound: hum resolves into a single soft tone, then cuts to silence.
- **0:18–0:20** — Hard cut to black. No CTA, no text. Just the brand mark (if available) or nothing at all — let it end on mystery.

**On-screen text:** Minimal — one line only, ivory serif on black, generous letter-spacing. No captions/hashtags overlaid on the visual itself.

**Sound direction:** Original low-frequency drone/hum, sound-designed (not musical composition, not an album track) — think resonant tone generator, not a beat. No lyrics, no melody that could be mistaken for a song.

**Visual assets required:** Sacred-geometry motion loop (Flower of Life, thin gold stroke animation), black backdrop plate, subtle skin/shadow texture plate for the reveal moment. All per `BRAND_BIBLE.md` §6–7.

## Video 2 — "Gold in the Dark" (POV / atmosphere loop)

**Hook (0–3s):** Total darkness, then a single point of warm gold light ignites off-center and holds.

**Beat-by-beat script:**
- **0:00–0:02** — Black screen, silence.
- **0:02–0:05** — A single warm gold light source ignites in negative space (off-center, per BRAND_BIBLE symmetry/negative-space rules) — think a candle-flare or lens-flare bloom. Faint ambient frequency-ring pulse begins under it, like a heartbeat made visible (concentric gold rings emanating slowly).
- **0:05–0:12** — Camera (or simulated push) drifts slowly toward the light — deliberate, unhurried motion, not a snap-zoom. Rings continue pulsing outward in time with the ambient tone. On-screen text appears, centered, small: *"Can You Feel The Frequency?"*
- **0:12–0:16** — Text holds on screen, light continues to pulse. No further text, no logo yet.
- **0:16–0:18** — Slow fade to black. Final pulse ring fades last, like an afterimage.

**On-screen text:** Uses the primary slogan exactly as written — "Can You Feel The Frequency?" — as the sole on-screen line, presented as an intimate question, not a shout. No exclamation points.

**Sound direction:** Original ambient frequency tone (soft pulse synced to the ring animation) — sound-design only, explicitly not a snippet of any album track. Consider a binaural-style tone for headphone viewers to reinforce "frequency" conceptually.

**Visual assets required:** Light-bloom/lens-flare render or practical light plate, concentric frequency-ring motion graphic, black backdrop. Ensure the ring motif still reads at thumbnail scale.

## Video 3 — "Fragments" (trend-adjacent: silent transition format)

**Trend adapted:** The widely used "silent-to-reveal" transition trend (subject holds stillness through a hard cut/transition beat, common in aesthetic/mood TikToks). **Brand-safe treatment:** strip out the fast-cut, high-energy music and comedic timing typical of the trend; replace with a single slow geometric transition and silence-to-tone shift, preserving the "reveal" structure without any hype pacing, trend audio, or album audio.

**Hook (0–3s):** Static black-and-gold frame holds completely still for a beat longer than a viewer expects — creating tension before the transition.

**Beat-by-beat script:**
- **0:00–0:04** — Static shot: a Metatron's Cube motif rendered faintly in gold, centered on black, holding still. Ambient near-silence (very faint room tone only).
- **0:04–0:05** — Sharp, single transition beat (one soft percussive "tick" — original sound design, not a drum loop) — geometry snaps to a slightly different, more resolved configuration (lines complete that were previously incomplete).
- **0:05–0:09** — Hold on the new, more complete geometry. Faint tone begins under it.
- **0:09–0:14** — On-screen text fades in: *"The World Is Changing..."* (exact primary slogan, ellipsis intact).
- **0:14–0:17** — Hold, then fade to black.

**On-screen text:** Primary slogan only, ivory/gold serif, centered, generous spacing.

**Sound direction:** One designed transition "tick" + sustained ambient tone underneath — original, non-musical. No trend audio track used (even trend audio, if licensed music, risks conflicting with "no album audio" spirit and off-brand energy).

**Visual assets required:** Two-state Metatron's Cube render (incomplete → more complete), transition sound effect, black plate.
