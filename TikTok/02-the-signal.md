## Video 1 — "Decode This" (fragment + speculation bait, brand-safe)

**Hook (0–3s):** Fast on-screen text flash — "Something is being encoded." — over a spiral of gold light forming mid-frame.

**Beat-by-beat script:**
- **0:00–0:03** — Black frame. Golden-ratio spiral begins tracing itself, slow and deliberate (per motion rules — no fast/chaotic cutting). On-screen text (upper third): *"Something is being encoded."*
- **0:03–0:09** — Spiral completes one full rotation; as it does, a faint, unidentified rhythmic pulse enters underneath the ambient tone — NOT a beat/groove, just an irregular pulse pattern (like Morse-code-adjacent ticks), clearly sound-designed rather than musical.
- **0:09–0:15** — Text updates, centered: *"Twenty-one signals. One awakening."* (supporting language bank line from `BRAND_BIBLE.md` §2 — used verbatim, no invented copy).
- **0:15–0:19** — Spiral fades, pulse fades with it. Final on-screen text, small: *"Can You Feel The Frequency?"*
- **0:19–0:21** — Cut to black.

**On-screen text:** Uses only pre-approved language bank / primary slogan lines — nothing invented. No numeric claims beyond "twenty-one," which is already confirmed (`CLAUDE.md` §3 track count).

**Sound direction:** Original irregular pulse/tick pattern + sustained ambient drone underneath — designed specifically to sound like "a signal," not a song fragment. Explicitly not a rhythm loop or groove (would risk reading as an Afrobeat instrumental preview).

**Visual assets required:** Golden-ratio spiral motion render, pulse/tick sound design asset, ambient drone bed.

**Comment-bait design note:** The vague, symbolic copy is intentionally interpretive — inviting speculation in comments ("what is this?") without resorting to clickbait phrasing. Community management should respond in-voice (mysterious, warm) rather than confirming/denying.

## Video 2 — "Frequency Made Visible" (waveform / ASMR-adjacent)

**Hook (0–3s):** Extreme close-up of a gold ring rippling outward in total silence — then the first sound hits exactly as the ring completes.

**Beat-by-beat script:**
- **0:00–0:02** — Extreme close-up, black background, single point of gold light. Total silence (use platform's native silence, not muted audio, so the sound hit lands with impact).
- **0:02–0:03** — A concentric frequency ring pulses outward from the point — synced precisely to a single soft tone (ASMR-adjacent precision, ear-catching but not jarring).
- **0:03–0:12** — Multiple rings pulse in sequence, each with its own soft original tone, gradually building a simple ambient tone-scape (still not musical/rhythmic — closer to a tuning-fork or singing-bowl quality). Camera slowly pulls back to reveal the rings are emanating from a hand or silhouette (partially lit, rest in shadow).
- **0:12–0:17** — On-screen text fades in, lower third: *"A frequency for those who are ready to listen."* (language bank line, verbatim).
- **0:17–0:20** — Rings fade to stillness, tone fades to silence, hold on black for a beat before cut.

**On-screen text:** Language bank line only, ivory sans-serif, wide tracking per typography rules.

**Sound direction:** Layered original tuning-tone/singing-bowl-style sound design, mixed for headphone ASMR-style clarity — explicitly avoids drum patterns, basslines, or any musical structure that could be mistaken for Afrobeat instrumentation.

**Visual assets required:** Frequency-ring motion graphic (multiple layered rings), silhouette/hand plate with rim lighting, tuning-tone sound design.

**Hook (0–3s):** A Merkaba (two interlocking tetrahedra) rotates slowly into frame from total black, catching a single light source.

**Beat-by-beat script:**
- **0:00–0:04** — Merkaba geometry rotates into view, slow, deliberate (matches "used sparingly, for symbolically weighted moments" per `BRAND_BIBLE.md` §6 — appropriate here as an early, rare use to mark this as a significant fragment).

- **0:10–0:15** — On-screen text, centered: *"The World Is Changing..."*
- **0:15–0:19** — Text holds; geometry keeps a very slow, almost imperceptible rotation (never fully static, echoing "unfolding, not completed" per slogan usage notes).
- **0:19–0:21** — Fade to black, no hard CTA text overlaid (caption below the video, not on-screen, can gently invite interpretation — see caption note).

**On-screen text:** Primary slogan only, exact wording, ellipsis intact.

**Sound direction:** Reprise of the tuning-tone ambient bed from Video 2 at reduced intensity — builds sonic continuity/recognizability across Phase 2 without ever becoming musical or rhythmic.

**Visual assets required:** Merkaba motion render (rare/reserved use — confirm this is an acceptable early use per brand governance, since Brand Bible flags Merkaba for "most symbolically weighted moments"), ambient tone asset reused from Video 2.

**Caption note (below video, not on-screen):** Something like "What do you see in it?" — an invitation, not a command — consistent with §6 "avoid aggressive imperative commands."
