# Phase 6 — Release Week (2 August – 8 August 2026)

**Cadence:** Daily on TikTok, 1–2x on key days. **Album audio:** now permitted — this is the first phase where real album audio may be used, starting exactly at release (2 August 2026). Before the drop moment on 2 August, treat content the same as Phase 5 (no audio); from the drop moment onward, album audio is allowed and encouraged. **Album mention:** full release — center the audience's first-listen experience and reaction, since there were no pre-release singles, per `RELEASE_PLAN.md` Phase 5 and `CONTENT_CALENDAR.md` §3 (Phase 6 goals). Must be synchronized with Spotify/Apple Music going live and with Instagram/Facebook/YouTube within the same 24-hour window.

Below are 4 representative video concepts: the release-moment drop, a track-specific first-listen framing, a UGC-style reaction format, and a first-week recap.

**Hook (0–3s):** The 21-node ring from Phase 5, all nodes lit — then, for the first time in the campaign, real sound plays under it: a brief passage of actual album audio.

**Beat-by-beat script:**
- **0:00–0:03** — Visual callback: fully-lit 21-node ring on black, silent for the first split second (final callback to the withheld-until-now tension).
- **0:03–0:04** — Hard cut: the ring "releases" — light blooms outward from the geometry into gold — and real album audio begins (a short, well-chosen passage from one of the 21 tracks — production/marketing team selects; should feel like an arrival, not necessarily the most "single-like" hook, since there are no singles).
- **0:04–0:15** — Album cover art full-frame, audio continues, on-screen text: *"The Awakening Code is here."*
- **0:15–0:20** — Text: *"Twenty-one tracks. All at once. Everywhere."*
- **0:20–0:26** — Text: *"Stream it now — link in bio."* (Calm CTA, not urgent/shouty, consistent with brand voice even on release day.)
- **0:26–0:28** — Closing slogan card: *"The World Is Changing... Can You Feel The Frequency?"*

**On-screen text:** Straightforward release-day facts and CTA — no invented streaming numbers or claims ("biggest release," "#1," etc.) unless/until real, verifiable data exists.

**Sound direction:** First and most significant use of real album audio in the entire campaign — select the passage carefully with the artist/label; still cut and mixed with the same restraint and intentionality as the rest of the brand (avoid a jarring, chaotic edit even though this is the highest-energy moment of the campaign).

**Visual assets required:** Final album cover art, 21-node "release" bloom animation, selected album audio passage (rights-cleared, approved by artist/label), link-in-bio CTA graphic.

## Video 3 — "React With Us" (UGC / duet trend, now brand-safe with real audio)

**Trend adapted:** The "reaction duet" format — creators film their real-time reaction to hearing something for the first time, inviting others to duet with their own reaction. **Brand-safe treatment:** this trend, which was off-limits pre-release (would have required previewing unreleased audio), becomes fully usable in Phase 6 since album audio is now cleared for use — no adaptation needed to avoid audio rules, but keep the reaction itself composed/genuine rather than exaggerated or comedic, to preserve the cinematic tone even in a reaction format.

**Hook (0–3s):** A genuine, composed reaction shot (lit per brand rules where possible — warm, low-key, not a harsh ring-light selfie aesthetic) as a track plays for the first time.

**Beat-by-beat script:**
- **0:00–0:03** — Subject listening with headphones, eyes closed or gaze middle-distance, warm gold key light, black background — composed, not manic. Real album audio plays under it (a track passage chosen for emotional impact).
- **0:03–0:15** — Held reaction, subtle real emotion, no jump cuts or exaggerated mugging for camera — the brand's "cinematic stillness" rule applies even to a reaction format. On-screen text, small: *"Duet your first listen."*
- **0:15–0:18** — Cut to black, text: *"The Awakening Code — out now everywhere."*

**On-screen text:** Invitation-style CTA ("Duet your first listen"), not an imperative demand, consistent with `BRAND_BIBLE.md` §3 tone rules.

**Sound direction:** Real album audio, artist/label-selected passage; this is the first video format that leans on user-generated amplification, so audio choice should be emotionally strong/shareable within the album's actual content (not treated as a "single," simply a well-chosen moment of the full body of work).

**Visual assets required:** Reaction footage (real subject, brand-lit), selected album audio passage.
