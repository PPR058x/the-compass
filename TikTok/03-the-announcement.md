**Cadence:** 4x/week on TikTok. **Album audio:** still not permitted — this phase reveals title/cover/date/concept, not music. **Album mention:** full reveal — title, cover art, release date, track count (21), core concept, explicit "no lead singles" framing, pre-save/pre-add CTA. Must land within the same 24-hour window as Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube per `CLAUDE.md` §8 cross-platform rule.

Below are 4 representative video concepts covering the announcement beat, the "no singles" framing beat, the pre-save CTA, and a press-adjacent anchor piece — illustrating the phase's higher cadence and reveal-focused goals.

- **0:14–0:19** — Second title card: **"21 Tracks. One Awakening. August 2, 2026."** (Uses confirmed facts only: track count and release date per `CLAUDE.md` §3.)
- **0:19–0:24** — Final card, centered: *"The World Is Changing... Can You Feel The Frequency?"* — both slogans paired per their "call-and-response" usage note in `BRAND_BIBLE.md` §2.
- **0:24–0:27** — Soft fade out on the tone; hold on gold logo/mark if available, else hold on black.

**On-screen text:** Album title, artist name, confirmed facts (21 tracks, 2 August 2026), both primary slogans paired exactly as documented.

**Sound direction:** Swelling original ambient/frequency-tone composition building to a resolved "arrival" moment — explicitly sound-designed, not a piece of album scoring or instrumentation. **Visual assets required:** Final album cover art (per `BRAND_BIBLE.md`/`ALBUM.md` — confirm final art is locked before this posts), fully-resolved Metatron's Cube render, title card typography per §5, swelling tone composition.

## Video 3 — "Pre-Save Is Open" (CTA / trend-adjacent: transformation reveal)

**Trend adapted:** The "outfit/room transformation" reveal format (before/after on a hard beat drop), extremely common on TikTok. **Brand-safe treatment:** replace the "beat drop" with the resolving chime of the original frequency tone, and replace the flashy after-state with restrained gold light rather than maximalist visual change — keeps the reveal mechanic (satisfying before/after) without the trend's usual fast-cut editing or licensed trend audio.

**Hook (0–3s):** A pre-save button/UI mockup sits dim and gray/inactive on screen — on-screen text: *"Not yet."*

**Beat-by-beat script:**
- **0:00–0:03** — Dim, desaturated mockup of a pre-save button (styled to still feel premium — no harsh UI chrome). Text: *"Not yet."*
- **0:03–0:04** — Single resolving chime (original tone, not a trend sound). Hard cut.
- **0:04–0:09** — Same button, now rendered in full gold-on-black brand style, glowing, "activated." Text: *"Now."*
- **0:09–0:15** — Cut to album cover with pre-save CTA overlay: *"Pre-save The Awakening Code — link in bio."* Ambient tone continues under it.
- **0:15–0:19** — Closing card: *"The World Is Changing..."*
- **0:19–0:21** — Fade out.

**On-screen text:** Direct CTA is permitted here (pre-save is a milestone action, not urgency/hype) — keep it factual and calm: "Pre-save The Awakening Code — link in bio," no countdown/urgency language, per `CLAUDE.md` §6.

**Sound direction:** Single resolving chime as the "transformation" beat, original tone-design — deliberately not a trend-audio drop.

**Visual assets required:** Pre-save UI mockup (dim + activated states), album cover art, link-in-bio graphic overlay per platform specs.
