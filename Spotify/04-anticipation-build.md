## Canvas Concept Briefs

Continuing as campaign-moment templates (not per-track — tracklist still unconfirmed). Four briefs, one per brand pillar, echoing the "deeper dives into individual brand pillars" content goal in `CONTENT_CALENDAR.md` §3. Concept only — for Creative Director / video production review.

**1. "Frequency" pillar**
- Motion: a single gold ring pulses outward in rhythm, steady and even, never accelerating.
- Geometry element: concentric circles / radiating frequency rings.
- Color: Obsidian Black `#0A0A0A` ground, Signature Gold `#D4AF37` ring, faint Ember Gold `#B8860B` afterglow trail.
- Duration feel: 5-second loop, metronomic and calm.

**2. "Awakening" pillar**
- Motion: a point of light at center slowly brightens from a single pixel to a soft glow, then holds — no explosion, no flare.
- Geometry element: golden ratio spiral, barely visible, tracing the light's growth.
- Color: black ground, Radiant Champagne `#E8D9A0` glow core.
- Duration feel: 6–7 second loop, unhurried.

**3. "Unity" pillar**
- Motion: multiple thin gold lines converge from the frame's edges toward a shared center point, arriving together, not competing.
- Geometry element: Flower of Life, rendered as convergence lines rather than the full completed pattern.
- Color: black ground, Signature Gold `#D4AF37` lines.
- Duration feel: 6-second loop.

**4. "Light" pillar**
- Motion: a single light ray sweeps slowly across a dark field, catching faint geometry as it passes, then fades.
- Geometry element: implied sacred geometry, only visible where the light ray touches it.
- Color: black ground, warm gold ray, no cool tones.
- Duration feel: 7-second loop.
