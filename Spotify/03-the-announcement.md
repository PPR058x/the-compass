## Canvas Concept Briefs

Canvas is track-attached on Spotify, and the 21-track tracklist is not yet finalized (see `ALBUM.md`) — so these are **style templates tied to campaign moments**, not per-track assets. They establish the visual language now so that whichever tracks are later assigned Canvas loops stay on-brand. Concept only — not a final asset; hand to Creative Director / video production for execution.

**1. "Metatron's Cube Emergence" (Announcement moment)**
- Motion: thin gold Metatron's Cube lines assemble themselves out of darkness, line by line, converging to a single point of light.
- Geometry element: Metatron's Cube (per `BRAND_BIBLE.md` §6 — "the code itself").
- Color: Obsidian Black `#0A0A0A` background, Signature Gold `#D4AF37` linework, a soft Radiant Champagne `#E8D9A0` glow at the convergence point.
- Duration feel: 6–8 second seamless loop, slow and deliberate — no hard cuts, no fast assembly.
- Pairing: works underneath the slogan pairing "The World Is Changing... / Can You Feel The Frequency?" if adapted for a title card, but as Canvas it should run silent-suited (no on-screen text — Canvas loops without text overlays performs better and stays cinematic).

**2. "Pre-Save Pulse" (secondary template, optional)**
- Motion: a single frequency ring emanates outward from center in a slow pulse, fading before completing the frame edge.
- Geometry element: concentric circles / radiating frequency rings.
- Color: same palette as above; ring rendered in Deep Bronze Gold `#8A6E2F` fading to transparent.
- Duration feel: 4–6 second loop, meditative pacing.
