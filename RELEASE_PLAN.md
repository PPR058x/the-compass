# Release Plan — The Awakening Code

**Release Date:** 2 August 2026
**Artist:** Patrick Pascal Reerink
**Genre:** Afrobeat
**Strategy:** Full album launch — no lead singles released ahead of the album.

---

## Philosophy

This is not a countdown-and-hype release plan. The campaign unfolds like a ritual: a signal is sent, it builds, and it culminates in a single, complete revelation — all 21 tracks arriving at once, as one unified frequency rather than a string of pre-release singles.

Withholding the music entirely until release day is itself a brand statement: *The Awakening Code* is not sold to the audience piece by piece — it is delivered whole, the way a code is only meaningful once it's complete. Every phase should feel like an invitation deepening over time, echoing: *"The World Is Changing... Can You Feel The Frequency?"* — without urgency tactics, fear-based hooks, or "don't miss out" language.

---

## Phase 1 — The Signal (Awareness / Teaser)
**Suggested window:** T-8 weeks to T-5 weeks before release

**Goal:** Introduce the frequency without revealing the full picture. Build symbolic intrigue.

- Reveal brand world: black-and-gold visual identity, sacred geometry motifs, first use of primary slogans.
- No direct "album coming" announcement yet — plant atmosphere and symbolism first.
- Light audio teasers (sound design, frequency tones, brief unidentified clips) — never a preview of any actual track.
- Begin building an email/SMS or close-community list for those who want early access ("the ones who feel it first").

**Key asset needs:** brand key art, teaser video/reel concept, sacred-geometry motion loop, slogan reveal post.

---

## Phase 2 — The Announcement
**Suggested window:** T-5 weeks to T-3 weeks before release

**Goal:** Formally announce *The Awakening Code* — title, release date, concept — while confirming no lead singles will precede it.

- Official album announcement across all platforms simultaneously.
- Reveal album title, cover art, release date, track count (21), and core concept/narrative.
- Frame the "no pre-release singles" approach as intentional: the album arrives complete, as one transmission.
- Open pre-save / pre-add on Spotify and Apple Music.
- Begin press outreach (see `Press/`) — pitch feature stories on the concept, not just the release.
- Introduce 1–2 "anchor" pieces of content that explain the *why* behind the album (artist statement, short film, or long-form caption).

**Key asset needs:** announcement key art, pre-save landing content, press one-sheet, artist statement.

---

## Phase 3 — Anticipation Build
**Suggested window:** T-3 weeks to T-1 week before release

**Goal:** Deepen anticipation and story without releasing any music. Build the world further, not the tracklist piece by piece.

- Behind-the-scenes and making-of content (studio, process, creative intent) — not the songs themselves.
- Deeper dives into individual brand pillars (Frequency, Awakening, Unity, Light) through visuals, short essays, and symbolism.
- Continue press push using the concept and full-album approach as the hook for feature coverage.
- Optional: one extended trailer or short film (visual/cinematic, no album audio) that captures the album's emotional arc.

**Key asset needs:** behind-the-scenes footage, extended trailer/short film, concept deep-dive social content, continued press materials.

---

## Phase 4 — Final Countdown
**Suggested window:** T-2 weeks to release day

**Goal:** Reveal the full tracklist and bring the campaign to its release-day culmination — restrained, symbolic, not urgency-driven.

- Full 21-track tracklist reveal.
- Press embargo lifts — reviews and features go live in the days before release.
- Final countdown content framed as "the pattern completing," not "time running out."
- Confirm release-day plan across all platforms and with press contacts.

**Key asset needs:** tracklist reveal graphic/video, countdown motif assets (geometry completing), press review/quote graphics.

---

## Phase 5 — Release Week (The Awakening)
**Suggested window:** Release day (2 August 2026) through T+1 week

**Goal:** Deliver a unified, cinematic release moment across every channel at once — the entire 21-track album arriving in full, everywhere, simultaneously.

- Release-day rollout: full album live on Spotify and Apple Music, YouTube content (visualizers / possible short film), synchronized social posts across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok.
- Press: release-day coverage, interviews, possible listening event or livestream moment.
- Direct communication to the early-access list from Phase 1 — they receive the album first or alongside general release.
- Because there were no pre-release singles, release day is the audience's *first* listen — content should center that first-listen experience and reaction.

**Key asset needs:** final album art, full press kit, release-day social assets, platform release-day copy.

---

## Phase 6 — Sustained Frequency (Post-Release)
**Suggested window:** T+1 week to T+8 weeks after release

**Goal:** Keep the movement alive after the release moment — sustain rather than spike-and-drop. This is also the first point at which individual tracks can be spotlighted as focus tracks, since none were released as singles beforehand.

- Rolling content: lyric visuals, behind-the-scenes/making-of material, listener reactions and testimonials, deeper dives into individual tracks/themes.
- Consider spotlighting one or two standout tracks as "focus tracks" 3–4 weeks post-release to re-engage algorithms and audience attention — framed as discovery within the completed work, not a delayed single.
- Continue press follow-up (reviews, retrospective features, playlist placements).
- Begin planning next-phase content (visualizer series, remix/collab content, or live performance material) to extend the album's life cycle.

**Key asset needs:** ongoing content calendar entries, press follow-up list, performance/behind-the-scenes footage.
