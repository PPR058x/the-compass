01 — Artist

Biography

PATRICK PASCAL REERINK
Artistically known as PPR058x

Patrick Pascal Reerink is an independent electronic music artist and visionary creator from the Netherlands, creating music under his own name with PPR058x as his distinctive artistic signature.

His sound moves between electronic music, Afro-inspired rhythms, deep house, melodic atmospheres and conscious storytelling. His work explores themes of awakening, identity, perception, transformation and the search for deeper meaning.

At the heart of his artistic universe is The Compass — a musical journey designed to challenge perception and invite listeners to explore the space between what they know and what they have yet to discover.

Through hypnotic rhythms, cinematic textures, emotional melodies and conceptual storytelling, Patrick creates music that is meant to be more than entertainment — it is an experience, a reflection and a journey inward.

The Compass is the signal.
The music is the map.
You choose the direction.
