# Content Calendar — The Awakening Code

**Campaign window:** 4 May 2026 (T-90) → 1 September 2026 (T+30)
**Release date:** 2 August 2026 (T0)
**Strategy:** Full album launch — no lead singles released ahead of the album. All 21 tracks arrive at once on release day.

This document defines the **structure** of the campaign — phases, cadence, goals, platform roles, milestones, and asset needs. It does not yet contain captions, scripts, or post copy; those will be generated per-platform once this structure is approved, using `BRAND_BIBLE.md` for voice and visual rules.

| Phase | Name | Dates | Duration | Core Idea |

## 2. Posting Frequency Matrix

Frequency ramps up steadily from silence → signal → daily presence at release, then tapers into a sustainable post-release rhythm. No music is streamed or previewed before Phase 6.

| Phase | Instagram | Facebook | TikTok | YouTube | Spotify / Apple Music |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Foundation | 2x/week | 1x/week | 2x/week | — | — |
| 2. The Signal | 3x/week | 2x/week | 3–4x/week | 1 teaser video (no audio from the album) | — |
| 3. The Announcement | 4x/week | 3x/week | 4x/week | 1 major upload (announcement trailer) | Pre-save / pre-add opens |
| 4. Anticipation Build | 4x/week | 3x/week | 4–5x/week | 1 upload (behind-the-scenes / extended trailer) | Pre-save / pre-add reminders only — no release |
| 5. Final Countdown | Daily | 4x/week | Daily | 1–2 uploads (tracklist reveal, trailer) | Countdown reminders |
| 6. Release Week | Daily (2x key days) | Daily | Daily (1–2x) | Full release content | Full album live day 1 |
| 7. Sustained Frequency | 3x/week, tapering | 2x/week | 3x/week, tapering | 1x/week | Ongoing pitching/playlisting; focus-track pushes |

**Notes:**
- Spotify and Apple Music carry no release activity until Phase 6 — their earlier cadence is milestone-driven only (pre-save, pre-add, reminders).
- TikTok carries the highest raw frequency because short-form, sound-led content is where "frequency" as a concept translates most naturally to discovery — using original sound design and atmosphere, not album audio.
- No platform should exceed the frequencies above during Phases 1–4 — restraint is part of the brand's premium, unhurried identity, and reinforces that the album is being withheld intentionally until it's complete.

## 3. Content Goals by Phase

**Phase 1 — Foundation**
- Establish visual world (black-and-gold, sacred geometry) with zero direct album promotion.
- Build pattern-recognition: audience begins noticing a consistent aesthetic before knowing what it's for.
- Goal metric focus: aesthetic consistency, early saves/shares of atmosphere content, follower quality over quantity.

**Phase 2 — The Signal**
- Introduce fragments — geometry in motion, frequency/sound-wave imagery, partial slogans — without full context.
- Begin light audio teasing (original tones, unidentified sound fragments) — never a preview of an actual track.
- Goal metric focus: engagement rate, comments speculating/interpreting, early community formation.

**Phase 3 — The Announcement**
- Deliver the full reveal: album title, cover art, release date, track count (21), concept framing, primary slogans in full.
- Clearly communicate the full-album approach: no lead singles, the work arrives whole on 2 August 2026.
- Open pre-save (Spotify) and pre-add (Apple Music); direct all traffic there.
- Launch press outreach in parallel (see `Press/`).
- Goal metric focus: pre-save conversions, press pickups, reach/impressions spike.

**Phase 4 — Anticipation Build**
- Deepen the concept and story without releasing any music — behind-the-scenes, process, symbolism, artist perspective.
- Explore each brand pillar (Frequency, Awakening, Unity, Light) through standalone content pieces.
- Sustain press relationships and continue pitching the "complete work" angle.
- Goal metric focus: content saves/shares, press relationship depth, sustained (not spiking) engagement.

**Phase 5 — Final Countdown**
- Reveal full 21-track tracklist.
- Lift press embargo — reviews and features go live.
- Daily-cadence content builds toward release without urgency/fear tactics — countdown framed as "the pattern completing," not "time running out."
- Goal metric focus: pre-save total, anticipation engagement (shares, saves), press coverage volume.

**Phase 6 — Release Week**
- Deliver a synchronized release moment across Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and press simultaneously — the audience's first listen to any part of the album.
- Center audience first-listen experience and reaction, not just the artist's announcement.
- Goal metric focus: day-1 streams across all 21 tracks, cross-platform simultaneity, organic sharing/reaction content.

**Phase 7 — Sustained Frequency**
- Prevent post-release drop-off with rolling content: track-by-track deep dives, behind-the-scenes/making-of, listener reactions and testimonials.
- Introduce a "second moment" — one or two focus tracks spotlighted around T+21 — framed as discovery within the completed work, not a delayed single, to re-engage algorithms.
- Goal metric focus: catalog stream retention, follower growth curve, earned/organic content from listeners.

## 4. Platform Strategy

| Platform | Role | Content Mix Emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Primary visual storytelling home | Key art, carousels, reels, stories — the most brand-complete platform, carries full visual identity |
| **Facebook** | Community + longer-form + press cross-posting | Announcements, event hosting, longer captions, press links, older/broader-reach audience |
| **TikTok** | Discovery engine, sound-first | Short-form, frequency/atmosphere-driven, geometry-in-motion, original sound fragments, trend-adjacent but never off-brand |
| **YouTube** | Long-form + visualizer + archive home | Trailers, behind-the-scenes, short films, full tracklist reveal video, release-day full content |
| **Spotify** | Primary streaming + editorial | Pre-save, Canvas loops, playlist pitching, full-album release-day editorial push |
| **Apple Music** | Parallel streaming + editorial | Pre-add, editorial pitching, Motion artwork if available |
| **Press** | Third-party credibility | Concept-led feature pitching (Phase 3 onward), emphasizing the full-album/no-singles approach — see `Press/` |

**Cross-platform rule:** Every major milestone (announcement, tracklist reveal, release day) must land on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube within the same 24-hour window. Spotify/Apple Music changes (pre-save live, album live) should be the trigger event that social content responds to, not a follow-up. No platform previews any album audio before Phase 6.
