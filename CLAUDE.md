## 5. Tone of Voice

**The voice is:** premium, cinematic, warm, hopeful, mysterious, elegant, inviting — inspiring the audience to feel part of a movement.

**The voice is not:** hype-driven, salesy, fearful, negative, exaggerated, clickbait.

Every piece of copy should be able to sit next to *"The World Is Changing... Can You Feel The Frequency?"* without clashing in register. If it reads like typical social-media hype copy, it's off-brand — rewrite it.

---

## 6. Writing Style

- Spacious, deliberate sentences over rapid-fire hype phrasing.
- Let mystery and restraint create intrigue — don't over-explain.
- Center emotion, unity, and transformation — not scarcity, competition, or FOMO.
- Use "we" / "you" to build a shared journey; avoid aggressive imperative commands ("BUY NOW", "DON'T MISS OUT").
- No countdown/urgency language framed as pressure (e.g. "only X days left!"). Countdown framing, when used, is symbolic ("the pattern completing"), not transactional.
- Avoid excessive exclamation points, emoji stacking, or ALL CAPS shouting.
- Minimal, intentional emoji use only if platform-appropriate and still on-brand (e.g. a single gold/light-related symbol) — never as filler or excitement markers.

---

## 7. Visual Style

- Low-key, high-contrast lighting; subject emerges from darkness into a single warm gold light source.
- Cinematic stillness — contemplative, composed, dignified; avoid casual/candid snapshot aesthetics.
- Deep blacks, warm gold highlights only — no cool blues, neon, or desaturated grays in final grade.
- Symmetry and centered framing where possible; negative space is a feature.
- Motion content (reels/video): slow, deliberate movement — smoke, light rays, particles, lens flares. Avoid fast, chaotic cutting.
- Sacred geometry renders as thin gold strokes on black, feeling like it emerges from light — never as heavy filled shapes competing with the artist or typography.
- Full detail: `BRAND_BIBLE.md` sections 4–8.

## 8. Social Media Rules

- No album audio, streams, or previews before release day (2 August 2026) — no exceptions, no "just a snippet."
- No lead-single-style promotion at any point — this is a full-album campaign only.
- Every major milestone (announcement, tracklist reveal, release day) must land on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube within the same 24-hour window — no platform gets an exclusive early scoop on a milestone unless explicitly planned.
- Posting frequency and phase-by-phase content goals must follow `CONTENT_CALENDAR.md` — don't post ahead of or out of sequence with the current campaign phase without checking it first.
- Platform roles are fixed (see `CONTENT_CALENDAR.md` §4): Instagram = primary visual storytelling, Facebook = community/longer-form/press cross-posting, TikTok = short-form sound/atmosphere discovery, YouTube = long-form/visualizer/archive, Spotify/Apple Music = streaming + editorial pitching only (not organic "posts").
- Spotify and Apple Music content changes (pre-save live, album live) should be treated as trigger events that social content responds to — not an afterthought.

## 9. Content Quality Standards

- Every piece of content must trace back to at least one brand pillar (Frequency, Awakening/Transformation, Light, Unity) — if it doesn't, it doesn't belong in this campaign.
- No factual claims about the artist's biography, history, or achievements unless confirmed — placeholders over invention, always (see §2).
- No fabricated press quotes, review excerpts, streaming numbers, or achievements. Real quotes/reviews are only added after they actually exist (post embargo lift, Phase 5 in `RELEASE_PLAN.md`).
- Consistency over novelty: it is better for a post to feel "on-brand but familiar" than "fresh but off-brand."
- Every asset should hold up at small scale (e.g. social avatar/thumbnail) as well as large (key art) — verify geometry and typography remain legible when scaled down.
- Before finalizing any piece of content, it should pass this check: *does this feel like an invitation into a movement, or like something being sold?* If it reads like a sale, revise it.

## 10. Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Let black dominate; let gold arrive as light.
- Use sacred geometry as structure, not decoration.
- Write and design with restraint, warmth, and intention.
- Treat every post/asset as an invitation into a shared movement.
- Flag missing factual information with placeholders instead of guessing.
- Check `CONTENT_CALENDAR.md` for current phase before generating new content.

**Don't:**
- Use hype language, urgency tactics, or fear-based hooks.
- Preview, tease, or leak any album audio before 2 August 2026.
- Promote any track as a "single" before release — there are none.
- Overcrowd compositions — resist the urge to fill empty space.
- Mix in cool colors, neon, or off-brand palettes.
- Use more than two typefaces or more than one geometry motif per composition.
- Invent biographical facts, quotes, reviews, or statistics.
- Shift the release date (2 August 2026) without explicit instruction.

## 11. Decision-Making Guidelines for Future Content Generation

When generating new content (posts, scripts, prompts, press copy, etc.), work through this order:

1. **Check the phase.** Look up the current/target date in `CONTENT_CALENDAR.md` §1 and §5 to determine which campaign phase applies, and what's allowed/expected in it (e.g. no music before Phase 6).
2. **Check the platform role.** Confirm the content type matches the platform's defined role (§8 above / `CONTENT_CALENDAR.md` §4).
3. **Check brand alignment.** Confirm the content connects to at least one brand pillar and follows the palette, geometry, and imagery direction in `BRAND_BIBLE.md`.
4. **Check the voice.** Read the draft against §5–6 above — cut anything that reads as hype, urgency, or oversell.
5. **Check factual accuracy.** Flag anything biographical, statistical, or quote-based that isn't already confirmed in this project — use a placeholder, don't invent.
6. **Check consistency with existing content.** Avoid contradicting previously generated posts/press materials (e.g. don't reference a single that doesn't exist, don't reveal tracklist details before the confirmed reveal milestone).
7. When a request conflicts with any of the above (e.g. "post a preview of a track" before release day), surface the conflict and ask before proceeding rather than silently complying or silently refusing.
